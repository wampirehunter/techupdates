QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden costs?

None. What you see is what you pay. Website development is a one-time project fee. Hosting can be paid monthly or annually with no lock-in contracts.

How do I make payments if there is no payment gateway?

We work with transparent direct invoicing: Bank Transfer (IMPS/NEFT), UPI, or Wire Transfer with milestone payments (e.g. 50% advance upon project confirmation and 50% upon final sign-off and launch).

Can I use my existing domain name?

Yes! We will configure your existing domain (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, Hostinger, etc.) to point seamlessly to your new high-speed website and global CDN at no extra charge.
